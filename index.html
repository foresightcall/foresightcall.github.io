How it works

How we work out the chancesEvery team has a rating. It goes up after a win and down after a loss. For each match we compare the two ratings, give the home side a small boost, and turn the gap into a chance for each result.

Make your pickTap a home win, a draw or an away win before kickoff. You can change your mind until the match starts. Then it locks.

Earn pointsA right pick is worth 3 points. If you back a result we gave under 25%, it's worth 5.

Foresight Call is just for fun. No betting, no prizes, no money. Our chances are estimates, and plenty of matches go the other way.
